Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard – Excel
📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes marketing campaign performance using Microsoft Excel. The goal is to understand how different marketing channels and regions perform based on spending, revenue, conversions, profit, and other key performance indicators.

An interactive Excel dashboard was created to transform raw campaign data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven marketing decisions.

🎯 Problem Statement

Marketing teams run campaigns across multiple channels and regions, but raw campaign data can be difficult to interpret.

This project aims to answer questions such as:

Which marketing channel performs best?
Which region generates the highest revenue and profit?
How effectively is the marketing budget being utilized?
Which campaigns generate better returns?
What trends can help improve future marketing decisions?
🎯 Project Objectives
Clean and prepare marketing campaign data.
Analyze campaign performance using Excel.
Create meaningful KPIs.
Analyze channel-wise and region-wise performance.
Identify sales, conversion, and profitability trends.
Build an interactive Excel dashboard.
Generate actionable business recommendations.

📈 Key Results

The analysis of 500 marketing campaigns produced the following overall results:

KPI	Result
Total Budget	$3.31M
Total Spend	$3.13M
Total Revenue	$6.55M
Total Conversions	184K+
Total Profit	$3.42M
Overall ROAS	2.09
🔍 Key Insights
Channel Performance
Instagram achieved the highest ROAS at approximately 2.29.
Instagram also generated the highest number of conversions.
Facebook and Google Ads also showed strong overall performance.
Twitter had the lowest ROAS among the analyzed channels.
Regional Performance
West generated the highest revenue of approximately $1.75M.
West also generated the highest profit of approximately $942K.
South showed similarly strong performance with approximately $1.66M revenue and $891K profit.
Overall Performance
The campaigns generated more revenue than the total marketing spend.
The overall ROAS of 2.09 indicates that the campaigns generated approximately $2.09 in revenue for every $1 spent.
Channel and regional performance varied, providing opportunities for better budget allocation.
💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

Increase focus on high-performing channels such as Instagram.
Optimize spending on low-performing channels and review their campaign strategies.
Allocate more budget toward regions showing stronger ROAS and profitability.
Monitor ROAS, CPA, and conversion performance regularly.
Use monthly trends to identify periods of high and low campaign performance.
Continuously compare campaign performance to improve future marketing decisions.
